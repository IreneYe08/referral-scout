# LinkedIn referral outreach

Goal: build real relationships with people at target companies so referrals come naturally. Get the referral before applying, because applying first can void it.

## 1. Pick targets
- The owner names a company, or a scout routine sources candidates.
- For a market sweep, build a company map first. Cover stage, latest round, offices, open PM roles with their years-of-experience requirements and locations, and a fit tier (A/B/C).

## 2. Research (read-only)
- Company basics: product, funding, offices, recent news, and open roles with links.
- Find 5-6 people. Prefer peer-level PMs, people in the owner's city, alumni of the owner's schools, and people from the owner's background. Keep about 70% peers and 30% senior people or hiring managers.
- For each person, find a genuine hook with a source: a recent post, a blog, a shared school, or a career path. Confirm they still work there.
- Check the outreach log and exclude anyone already contacted.

## 3. Draft
- Write one personalized message per person.
- Connect notes stay under 300 characters, on one line, with no em dashes or arrows.
- Open with the owner's ownership and business scope, not small tasks. Add the hook, then a quick-chat ask, and end with the owner's portfolio link.
- Senior people get a "learning from you" framing, plus an offer to share what the owner sees firsthand.
- Never ask for a referral in the first message.

## 4. Owner review
- Show each person with why they're worth talking to and the exact draft.
- Ask with a multi-select. Send nothing without explicit approval.

## 5. Send and verify (browser)
- Open the profile and re-confirm the person's current company and the hook. If the hook turns out to be false, skip that person.
- Connect (it may be under More), then Add a note. Type the exact text, read it back, send once, and verify Pending.
- For a DM, click Message on the profile and check the thread first. The owner's own connect note in the thread is expected.
- If there's no Connect option or LinkedIn asks for an email address, skip that person and report it. Don't fall back to InMail unless the owner approved it.

## 6. Log and follow up
- Append every send to the outreach log with the date, person, channel and status.
- When someone accepts, draft a follow-up asking for a 15-20 min chat at the owner's preferred times, and get approval before sending it.
- Report send status honestly. Only say something is sent once it's verified.
