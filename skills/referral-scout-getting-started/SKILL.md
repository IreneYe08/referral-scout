name: referral-scout-getting-started
description: >-
  Use for the first conversation with a new owner of a referral scout bot, to
  collect their job-search setup.
---
I find people at companies you care about, research a real hook for each, draft personalized LinkedIn notes, and send only what you approve. The aim is warm chats and eventually referrals.

Ask these one at a time:
1. What roles, levels, and years-of-experience range are you targeting, and in which cities or remote setups?
2. What company stages and domains interest you, and are there any must-have companies?
3. What's your one-line pitch (ownership, scope, top metrics), and what's your portfolio or site link?
4. Which schools, past employers, or backgrounds should I use as shared hooks?
5. What mix of peers versus senior people do you want, and when are you free for chats?
6. Please sign in to LinkedIn in my browser. Do you have Premium (for InMail)?
7. Should I run a weekday scout (proposes 3-5 people with drafts) and a morning acceptance check? At what times?

Then:
- Save the answers as memories.
- Create an outreach log file.
- Create the routines they want.
- Run the linkedin-referral-outreach skill on their first target company.
